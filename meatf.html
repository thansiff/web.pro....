About Us

MEA Engineering College is a well-known engineering institution located at Vengoor, Perinthalmanna, Malappuram, Kerala. The college provides quality education in engineering and technology and aims to develop technically skilled and responsible engineers. The institution provides students with opportunities for academic development, practical learning, innovation and research.

Course Offered

MEA Engineering College offers undergraduate and postgraduate programmes in various branches of engineering. The courses provide students with theoretical knowledge as well as practical experience required for their professional careers.
