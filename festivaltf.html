INDIAN FESTIVALS

  • DIWALI

Diwali, also known as Deepavali, is the vibrant festival of lights celebrated by millions of Hindus, Jains, Sikhs, and some Buddhists across the world. It symbolizes the spiritual victory of light over darkness, good over evil, and knowledge over ignorance. In 2026, the main day of Diwali falls on Sunday, November 8.

  • ONAM

Onam is the official 10-day state and harvest festival of Kerala Tourism that celebrates the Malayali New Year and the legendary annual homecoming of the benevolent King Mahabali

  • HOLI

Holi is a major Hindu Holi spring festival known as the festival of colors and love.
