HOLI




Holi, Hindu spring festival celebrated in India and Nepal on the full-moon day of Phalguna (February–March). Participants throw colored water and colored powders on one another, and license is given to deviate from the usual societal norms and rankings of caste, gender, status, and age. In the streets the celebrations of revelers who “play” Holi (Hindi: Holi khelna) are often marked by loud music and shouts of “Holi hai” (Hindi: “It’s Holi!”), as well as ribald language and behavior, as layers of multiple colors transcend any overt or societal differences. But at its conclusion, when everyone bathes, dons clean clothes, and visits friends, teachers, and relatives, the ordered patterns of society are reasserted and renewed.
